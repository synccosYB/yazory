Yazory helps families keep everyday life together

Yazory is a family-support and donation-management platform developed and operated by Synccos Inc. It connects families, rabbinical representatives, community advocates, and supporters in one secure system.

Sign inContact us

What we provide

We receive support applications, protect family information, manage pledges and donations, and give authorized family administrators a transparent record of funds received and distributed.

Donations and payments

Yazory will accept one-time and recurring donations through a secure payment provider. Yazory does not store full card numbers or card security codes.

Fund distributions

After required approvals, funds may be distributed to families or vendors. The payment provider may require identity and bank-account verification from each recipient.

Clear information for donors

A donation is not a purchase of goods or services. A receipt confirms payment, but tax deductibility depends on applicable law and the donor's circumstances. Donors should consult their tax adviser.

Consent to receive SMS messages

How consent is obtained

During a telephone conversation, a Yazory staff member explains that messages may include call scheduling, pledge and donation updates, payment reminders, confirmations, and receipts. Message frequency varies, typically up to four messages per month. Message and data rates may apply. The staff member explains that the recipient may reply STOP to opt out or HELP for assistance and asks: “Do you agree to receive SMS messages from Yazory?” Only after the recipient gives an explicit “yes” is the consent date and time recorded and SMS messaging permitted.

Your choice

Consent is voluntary and is not a condition of making a donation or receiving service. Consent may be withdrawn at any time by replying STOP.
